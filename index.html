    The Story

    From the driveway to the workshop.

    Haztech AutoWorks started the hard way. Ricky turned up to driveways across Melbourne, fixing cars on the spot and earning trust one customer at a time. Three years of mobile work, built on top of four years inside Mercedes and European workshops.

    That reputation grew faster than a single van could keep up with, from emergency call outs to bringing a classic Mercedes that had sat for three years back to life. So Haztech is doing what every great mechanic eventually does: putting down roots. A proper workshop in Tullamarine, with more tools, more capability and the same straight talking service that got us here.
